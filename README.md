### 🎟️ Turnero Ucen

![Status](https://img.shields.io/badge/Status-Delivered-7B2CBF?style=flat-square&labelColor=0D0B14)
![Universidad Central](https://img.shields.io/badge/Universidad_Central-Coquimbo_Campus-3C096C?style=flat-square&labelColor=0D0B14)
![Internship](https://img.shields.io/badge/Operational_Internship-5A189A?style=flat-square&labelColor=0D0B14)

**Overview**
A web-based queue management system developed during my Operational Internship at Universidad Central (Coquimbo Campus). It was built to manage the high flow of people during the 2027 Admissions and Enrollment period.

**Problem it solves**
During enrollment, the university faced crowded hallways and disorganized lines. Staff had to call out students by voice, and students often missed their turn. This platform digitizes the waiting line, separates users by physical location (Tower B, Tower C) and automates both visual and audio calls.

**Key features**

- 🔐 **Secure operator panel:** per-desk interface (desks 1 to 9) protected by location-based passwords (e.g. Tower B – Floor 1).
- 🔔 **Flow control:** "Call next ticket" and "Call again" actions (re-call when someone is absent).
- 📺 **Display synchronization:** public TV screens update in real time with alerts and synthesized voice.
- 🛠️ **Supervisor tools:** sync manual counters or reset the daily count to zero.

### 🎟️ Turnero Ucen

![Status](https://img.shields.io/badge/Status-Delivered-8B0000?style=flat-square&labelColor=0D0D0D)
![Universidad Central](https://img.shields.io/badge/Universidad_Central-Coquimbo_Campus-1B1B2F?style=flat-square&labelColor=0D0D0D)
![Internship](https://img.shields.io/badge/Operational_Internship-00B4D8?style=flat-square&labelColor=0D0D0D)

**The tale**
A web-based queue management system built during my Operational Internship at Universidad Central (Coquimbo Campus). It was deployed to control the massive flow of people during the 2027 Admissions and Enrollment season.

**The curse it breaks**
The university faced chaos and crowded hallways during enrollment. Staff were losing their voices calling out to incoming students, who often missed their turn. This platform digitizes the waiting line, separates users by physical location (Tower B, Tower C) and automates both visual and audio calls.

**Powers**

- 🔐 **Secure operator panel:** per-desk interface (desks 1 to 9) protected by location passwords (e.g. Tower B – Floor 1).
- 🔔 **Flow control:** buttons to "Call next ticket" or "Call again" (re-call when someone is absent).
- 📺 **Screen synchronization:** public TV displays update in real time with alerts and synthesized voice.
- 🛠️ **Supervisor tools:** sync manual counters or reset the day back to zero.
